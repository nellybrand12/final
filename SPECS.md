# Hotel Résidence Madadjeu - Specifications

This document outlines the unique screens and their interactive elements for the Hotel Résidence Madadjeu UI/UX project.

## Screens Overview

### 1. Home
- **Hero Section:** Contains primary calls-to-action (CTAs) for booking or exploring rooms.
- **Navigation Menu:** Links to main sections (Rooms, Gallery, About, Contact, Manage Booking).
- **Language Toggle:** A switch allowing users to select between French (FR) and English (EN).
- **Floating CTA:** A persistent "Book Now" button in the bottom corner of the viewport.

### 2. Rooms & Suites
- **Room Grid:** A list or grid of available rooms/suites.
  - **Room Card:** Clickable elements that lead to the room detail view.
  - **Book Buttons:** Direct booking buttons on each room card.
- **Filters/Sorting:** Dropdowns to filter rooms by capacity, price, or type (e.g., Suite vs. Standard).

### 3. Suite Royale (Room Detail)
- **Image Gallery/Carousel:** Interactive slider to view multiple photos of the suite.
- **Booking Widget:**
  - Check-in and Check-out date pickers.
  - "Reserve this suite" CTA button.
- **Amenities List:** Icons and text detailing the suite's features.

### 4. Réserver (Booking Flow)
This is a multi-step booking process.
- **Step 1 (Dates & Guests):** Date pickers and a counter for adults/children.
- **Step 2 (Personal Info):** Form inputs for Name, Email, Phone number, and Special Requests.
- **Step 3 (Payment):** Form inputs for payment details (Credit Card).
- **Navigation Buttons:** "Next", "Previous", and final "Confirm Booking" buttons.

### 5. Confirmation
- **Success Message:** Displays booking reference number.
- **Action Buttons:** "Back to Home" and "Download Receipt" (PDF export) buttons.

### 6. Gérer ma réservation (Manage Booking)
- **Lookup Form:** 
  - Input field for Booking Reference.
  - Input field for Email Address.
  - "Find Booking" submit button.
- **Management Actions:** Once a booking is found, buttons to "Modify" or "Cancel" the reservation.

### 7. Gallery
- **Filter Tabs:** Categories such as "Rooms", "Exterior", "Dining", "Spa".
- **Lightbox Overlay:** Clicking an image expands it to full screen with Next/Previous navigation controls.

### 8. Amenities
- **Category Toggles:** Accordions or tabs to switch between amenity sections (e.g., Spa, Gym, Restaurant, Concierge).
- **Interactive Map/Layout (Optional):** To show where amenities are located.

### 9. Location
- **Interactive Map:** An embedded map (e.g., Google Maps) showing the hotel's location, allowing zooming and panning.
- **Action Buttons:** "Get Directions" linking to an external map service.

### 10. About
- **Navigation Links:** Internal links to team bios or hotel history.
- **Contact Us CTA:** A button directing the user to the contact form.

### 11. FAQ (Frequently Asked Questions)
- **Accordion Toggles:** Clickable questions that expand to reveal the answers and collapse when clicked again.

### 12. Contact
- **Contact Form:**
  - Input for Name.
  - Input for Email.
  - Textarea for Message.
  - "Send Message" submit button.
